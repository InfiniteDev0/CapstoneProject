# Image and asset credits

## Photos

The shop ships **without product or page photos**. Every product shows a blank photo box until the admin uploads a photo (Admin → Products → a product → Photos), and the home page hero and tiles show plain dark or grey panels until photos are added to `apps/api/src/main/resources/static/images/`.

When you add photos, list them here:

| File | Where it shows | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| *(none yet)* | | | |

Rules: use photos you took yourself or that are free to use (for example the Unsplash or Pexels licence), with **no visible brand logos**. Do not use Under Armour (or any other brand's) product photos, athlete photos, logos or videos.
